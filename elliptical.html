The lens is a singular isothermal ellipsoid with external shear, the lens of the EUCLID-SAGE simulations, using lenstronomy conventions (θE is the geometric mean of the Einstein radii along the major and minor axes). The dashed purple curve in the picture is the critical curve, det A = 0. In the box, the solid curve is the caustic and the dashed curve the cut: a background galaxy outside the cut has no image near the lens centre. Equations: model note.
