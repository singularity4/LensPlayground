LensPlayground

Move the pointer across the sky. The gold galaxy bends the light of the galaxies behind it.

A massive galaxy bends the light of galaxies far behind it into arcs, rings and multiple images. Move the lens and the background galaxy and see the light bend.

Circular lens

A round lens galaxy. Move the background galaxy and a ring splits into two arcs.

Elliptical lens

An elongated lens galaxy pulled by its neighbours. One background galaxy becomes four images.

The lens and the galaxies follow the model of the simulated images of EUCLID-SAGE, a benchmark for finding strong lenses in Euclid images: an isothermal lens with external shear and Sérsic galaxies, using lenstronomy conventions. The Einstein radius follows from the velocity dispersion of the lens galaxy and the distances.

LensPlayground is part of EUCLID-SAGE.
